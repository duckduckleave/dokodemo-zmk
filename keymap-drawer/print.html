1 / 3
How to read: center = tap and bottom = hold. Colored keys activate the matching layer. Combos: X + C = Escape, H + , = Enter, and Z + / = Caps Word.

DokoDemo — NavNum

2 / 3
NavNum: navigation and Ctrl shortcuts on the left, numpad on the right. Hold both inner thumbs for Fn. NavNum Lock latches the persistent number layer.

DokoDemo — Function, Media & Gaming

3 / 3
Function: Bluetooth profiles, media controls, brightness, volume, firmware actions, and F-keys. Hold the Print Screen key for Shift; Shift plus Bluetooth profile 1 clears the currently selected bond. Gaming uses plain QWERTY on the left; press . + / to enter or leave it.
